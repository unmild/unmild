Website information

The website’s own code does not set cookies, use browser storage, include analytics or advertising scripts, or provide account registration. Fonts, images, and scripts are served from the same website.

Delivering a website requires network requests. The hosting service may receive information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. This source project does not establish the live host’s logging or retention practices.

Contacting the team

Email links open your email application. If you contact support@unmild.in, your email service and the receiving email service process the message. The information you choose to send may include your name, email address, message, and attachments.

Send only what is needed to explain your question. Do not include API keys, passwords, payment details, or private project files. Contact us to ask about the handling or deletion of a support conversation.

External services and products

Links to Microsoft Store, product documentation, Discord, Instagram, and YouTube take you to separate services. Their own terms and privacy notices apply. This website does not process purchases or collect payment information.

Desktop products and AI model providers have their own data flows and permissions. Review their documentation and notices before using them. A statement about this website should not be read as a privacy claim about those products.

Questions and requests

Contact support@unmild.in with a privacy question or a request concerning information you have shared. Available rights depend on the law that applies to your situation.

We may update this notice when the website changes. The date above identifies this version.

Deployment review

Operator disclosures, hosting and email providers, retention periods, and applicable privacy requirements must be confirmed before this notice is used for a production deployment.
