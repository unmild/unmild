A few useful details.

What should I include in a support email?

Include the product name, your app and Windows versions, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. If useful, include the steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot with private information removed.

Where can I learn more about a product?

Each app has its own website: Unmild Designer for hardware design and Unmild Agent for software development. These are the places to explore features and setup details.
