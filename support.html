Common questions.

Where do I find setup information?

See the product documentation for Designer or Agent.

Where do I manage a purchase?

Purchases are handled by Microsoft Store. Use the Microsoft account used for the purchase to view your order and the Store’s available support options. This website does not process payments.

Why does Agent also need an API key?

The app and model usage are separate. Agent connects to a supported provider using your API key; that provider bills model usage.

Can I share an idea for a product?

Yes. Email the team or join the conversation on Discord. Tell us what you are trying to achieve and where the workflow gets in the way.

How do I ask a privacy question?

Email support@unmild.in. Our website privacy notice explains the scope of this site.
