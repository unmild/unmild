Our philosophy

Useful by purpose.
Independent by choice.

UNMILD is an independent team creating software for people who build.

What drives the work

Less distance between
an idea and its execution.

Designing a board and developing software are different kinds of work. Both need tools that make the details clear, keep context within reach, and let you decide how to proceed.

That is the thread connecting Unmild Designer and Unmild Agent. We focus on practical workflows, careful design, and functionality you can put to use.

Our point of view

Software should
give you more control,
not more friction.

The principles

Built with intent.

01 / Independence

Your workflow comes first.

We build tools around the work people want to do. Choice and direct access should be useful parts of the experience.

02 / Craft

The small decisions matter.

How a control is named. Where a setting lives. Whether a result is easy to inspect. These details shape the experience of using software every day.

03 / Clarity

Say what a tool does.

We prefer specific capabilities, real screenshots, and clear requirements. Our products should be understandable before you install them.

04 / Responsibility

Be deliberate about access.

Privacy and permissions deserve careful attention. We explain the scope of this website in our privacy notice; each product’s own documentation covers its behavior.
