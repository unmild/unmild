Our perspective

Made for the part
where you make it real.

UNMILD makes desktop tools for people working on hardware and software. Our collection brings together Unmild Designer and Unmild Agent.

01 / Where we started

A board is a physical thing. Its dimensions matter. Its connections matter. So does being able to see what you are making.

That thinking sits at the heart of Unmild Designer: keeping PCB layout, physical review, and manufacturing output close together. The name UNMILD carries that hardware-first origin.

02 / What comes alongside

Software is another way to turn an idea into something people can use.

Unmild Agent brings the collection into software development, with a Windows workspace for AI-assisted tasks, project files, and tools. Different work, with the same interest in making the next step clearer.

03 / What we care about

Useful controls. Visible work. Enough room to think.

These are the qualities we want our tools to have. A clear view of the project, a way to inspect the details, and direct access to the things you need to change.
